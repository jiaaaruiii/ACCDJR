WAYS TO PASS BETWEEN SPACES

Keep these instructions. You may need them on the other side.

1×No tools needed

Use electronic devices

Screens can open into the scenes they show. Step into the TV when it plays the right scene, but check that you can land at that height.

Look for holes in space

Spaces have natural seams between them. When one splits open in a wall, you can simply walk through.

Use transport facilities

Everyday exits and vehicles can take you further than you think. Sometimes the lift doors open onto outer space.

Try different forms

A new form reveals paths that a body cannot use. Turn into water and you can leave through the sink.

Find the right angle

Some spaces are joined by optical illusions. The painted door only lines up from one spot, so find it before you walk.

Lie down, but don't fall asleep

The edge of sleep is a passage of its own. Stay half awake, and you may sink, bed and all, into somewhere else.

Check your blind spots

Space changes quietly wherever you are not looking. While you face forward, a new doorway may open just behind you.

Ask someone with special abilities

Some people can open passages that others cannot. Ask politely, and they may open a portal for you.

Lost between spaces?

Stay where you are. Most passages open again. Do not try to walk back through a closed one.
